# QCA6390 Bluetooth (umi)

QCA6390 Bluetooth over UART for the Xiaomi Mi 10 (umi, SM8250), with its RPMh rails and early start, plus the userland helper that hands the controller to iOS.

| Repository | Maintainer | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [ios-umi](https://github.com/qcom-toolbox/ios-umi) | [qcom-toolbox](https://github.com/qcom-toolbox) | [XNU patches 0234–0237](https://github.com/qcom-toolbox/ios-umi/tree/main/xnu/patches); [sweet-bt helper](https://github.com/qcom-toolbox/ios-umi/tree/main/phone/sweet-bt) |

## State

Scan, connect and pair in Settings; Control Center toggle. Bluetooth audio is untested. Device page: [Xiaomi Mi 10 — iOS 16](/devices/umi-qcom-toolbox).

## Maintenance

The maintainer owns the repository and its release decisions. UniXNU documentation lists the driver and its state; it does not take over maintenance.
