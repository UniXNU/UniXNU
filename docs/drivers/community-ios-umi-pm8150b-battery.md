# SweetBattery

PM8150B fuel gauge and charger control for the Xiaomi Mi 10 (umi, SM8250): `IOPMPowerSource` with real percentage, voltage, current and charging state; 4.40 V, 2.0 A charge current, input limit by charger type and QC 2.0 / 3.0 detection.

| Repository | Maintainer | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [ios-umi](https://github.com/qcom-toolbox/ios-umi) | [qcom-toolbox](https://github.com/qcom-toolbox) | [XNU patches 0157, 0252](https://github.com/qcom-toolbox/ios-umi/tree/main/xnu/patches) |

## State

Battery readings work; charging from a wall charger works. No USB-PD or 30 W fast charge. Device page: [Xiaomi Mi 10 — iOS 16](/devices/umi-qcom-toolbox).

## Maintenance

The maintainer owns the repository and its release decisions. UniXNU documentation lists the driver and its state; it does not take over maintenance.
